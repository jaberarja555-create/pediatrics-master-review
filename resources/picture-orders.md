## Chapter 1 · Topic 1.2 Developmental Milestones

**dev_moro.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3. A healthy newborn baby lying on its back on a white sheet showing the
Moro reflex: both arms flung outward and upward, elbows extended, fingers spread wide open, mouth open crying,
legs slightly flexed. Examiner's hands just behind the head. Plain background, no text.

**dev_atnr.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3, top view. A 2-month-old infant lying on its back on a white sheet in
the asymmetrical tonic neck reflex ("fencing") posture: head turned to the right, right arm and right leg extended,
left arm flexed with the hand near the head, left leg flexed. Plain background, no text.

**dev_headlag.jpg**
Two realistic medical photographs side by side, landscape 16:9, same examiner and clinic. Left: a newborn being
pulled to sit by the hands with complete head lag (head falling backward). Right: a 4-month-old being pulled to
sit with good head control (head in line with the trunk). Plain background, no text, no labels.

**dev_pincer.jpg** (optional)
Two realistic close-up photographs side by side, landscape 16:9. Left: a 6-month-old infant's hand holding a red
wooden cube with the whole palm (palmar grasp). Right: a 12-month-old infant picking up a single raisin between
the tip of the thumb and the tip of the index finger (pincer grip). Plain light table, no text.

**dev_parachute.jpg** (optional)
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3. An examiner holds a 10-month-old infant face-down by the trunk and
tilts the baby suddenly toward a padded table; the infant extends both arms forward with open hands to protect
itself (parachute reaction). Plain background, no text.

**dev_sitting.jpg** (optional)
Realistic photograph, square 1:1. A 6-month-old Sudanese baby sitting on a mat, leaning forward and propping
itself up on both hands placed on the floor in front (tripod sitting). Plain background, no text.
