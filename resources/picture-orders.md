# Picture orders (image prompts for Gemini)

Send each prompt as it is. Save the result with the file name given, and send the files (or a Word file with the
name under each picture). Rules for every picture: realistic, no text, labels, arrows or watermark in the image,
plain light background, clinic setting, Sudanese / East African mother and child, medical-textbook quality.

## Chapter 1 · Topic 1.1 Normal Growth & Growth Charts

**growth_length.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3. A Sudanese baby about 6 months old lying on its back on a wooden
infant length board (infantometer) on a clinic table. One health worker in a white coat holds the baby's head
against the fixed headboard; a second health worker straightens both knees with one hand and pushes the sliding
footboard against the flat soles with the other. Soft daylight, plain light background, no text, no labels.

**growth_ofc.jpg**
Realistic close-up medical photograph, landscape 4:3. A health worker measures the head circumference of a calm
Sudanese infant with a yellow non-stretch measuring tape passing just above the eyebrows and around the most
prominent part of the back of the head. Hands of the examiner visible, baby held by the mother. Plain background,
no text, no numbers readable on the tape.

**growth_hydrocephalus.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, portrait 3:4. An infant about 4 months old with hydrocephalus: a very large rounded
head out of proportion to the face, thin shiny scalp with prominent scalp veins, wide forehead, eyes deviated
downward with sclera visible above the iris (sunset sign). Infant lying on a white hospital sheet. Clinical,
respectful, no text.

**growth_microcephaly.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, portrait 3:4, side (profile) view. An infant about 6 months old with microcephaly:
a small head with a sloping forehead, the head clearly small compared with the face and body. Infant held upright
by the mother, plain background. Clinical, respectful, no text.

**growth_muac.jpg**
Realistic close-up medical photograph, landscape 4:3. A health worker measures the mid-upper arm circumference of
a thin Sudanese child about 18 months old with a coloured MUAC tape (red, yellow and green bands) wrapped around
the middle of the left upper arm; the window of the tape is on the red band. Arm hanging relaxed. Plain
background, no text or numbers readable.

**growth_achondroplasia.jpg** (optional)
Realistic medical photograph, portrait 3:4, full body, front view. A child about 5 years old with achondroplasia
standing: short upper arms and thighs (short limbs), normal-length trunk, large head with prominent forehead,
flat nasal bridge, short fingers. Wearing shorts and a T-shirt, plain light background, no text.

**Real pictures, not AI (AI draws them wrongly):**
- **growth_card.jpg**: a phone photo of the growth chart page of a real Sudan child health card (or the WHO

## Chapter 1 · Topic 1.2 Developmental Milestones

**dev_moro.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3. A healthy newborn baby lying on its back on a white sheet showing the
Moro reflex: both arms flung outward and upward, elbows extended, fingers spread wide open, mouth open crying,
legs slightly flexed. Examiner's hands just behind the head. Plain background, no text.

**dev_atnr.jpg**
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3, top view. A 2-month-old infant lying on its back on a white sheet in
the asymmetrical tonic neck reflex ("fencing") posture: head turned to the right, right arm and right leg extended,
left arm flexed with the hand near the head, left leg flexed. Plain background, no text.

**dev_headlag.jpg**
Two realistic medical photographs side by side, landscape 16:9, same examiner and clinic. Left: a newborn being
pulled to sit by the hands with complete head lag (head falling backward). Right: a 4-month-old being pulled to
sit with good head control (head in line with the trunk). Plain background, no text, no labels.

**dev_pincer.jpg** (optional)
Two realistic close-up photographs side by side, landscape 16:9. Left: a 6-month-old infant's hand holding a red
wooden cube with the whole palm (palmar grasp). Right: a 12-month-old infant picking up a single raisin between
the tip of the thumb and the tip of the index finger (pincer grip). Plain light table, no text.

**dev_parachute.jpg** (optional)
Realistic medical photograph, landscape 4:3. An examiner holds a 10-month-old infant face-down by the trunk and
tilts the baby suddenly toward a padded table; the infant extends both arms forward with open hands to protect
itself (parachute reaction). Plain background, no text.

**dev_sitting.jpg** (optional)
Realistic photograph, square 1:1. A 6-month-old Sudanese baby sitting on a mat, leaning forward and propping
itself up on both hands placed on the floor in front (tripod sitting). Plain background, no text.
