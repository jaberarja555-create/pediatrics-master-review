Data stations give a report (CSF, blood count, urine, blood gas) with a short case and ask for the abnormality, the diagnosis and the next step. Read the report in a fixed order, compare each value with its normal range, and then put the abnormal values together into one diagnosis.

Best next step

Present an X-ray in order: type of film and view, patient details, quality (rotation, inspiration, exposure), then the findings from the obvious abnormality outwards, and end with the diagnosis and the next investigation.
