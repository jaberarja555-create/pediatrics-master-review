Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) is an autosomal recessive defect of cortisol synthesis; in 90–95% the enzyme 21-hydroxylase is deficient. Low cortisol raises ACTH, the adrenals enlarge, and the blocked precursors are diverted to androgens. It is common where consanguinity is common, as in Sudan, and the salt-wasting form kills newborn boys if missed.

Investigations

  • 17-hydroxyprogesterone (17-OHP) ↑↑: diagnostic (after 48 h; ACTH test if borderline)
  • Na ↓, K ↑, glucose ↓, metabolic acidosis · ↑ renin, ↓ aldosterone
  • Karyotype (urgent), pelvic ultrasound (uterus)
  • Cortisol ↓, ACTH ↑, androgens ↑

Acute salt-wasting / adrenal crisis

  1. Shock: 0.9% saline 20 mL/kg bolus, repeat as needed, then maintenance + deficit with 0.9% saline in 5–10% dextrose
  2. Hypoglycaemia: 10% dextrose 2–5 mL/kg
  3. Hydrocortisone IV at once: 25 mg (infant), 50 mg (child), 100 mg (adolescent), or 2–4 mg/kg, then 50–100 mg/m²/day 6-hourly
  4. Hyperkalaemia: usually corrects with saline and hydrocortisone; treat if ECG changes (5.2)

Long-term treatment

GlucocorticoidHydrocortisone 10–15 mg/m²/day in 3 doses (suppresses ACTH and androgens) · avoid long-acting steroids in growing children
MineralocorticoidFludrocortisone 0.05–0.2 mg/day (salt-wasting)
SaltSodium chloride supplements in infancy (1–2 g/day)
Stress dosingDouble or triple the hydrocortisone dose during fever or illness; IM hydrocortisone if vomiting; emergency card/bracelet
MonitoringGrowth velocity, bone age (yearly), 17-OHP, androstenedione, renin, BP · over-treatment: Cushingoid, poor growth · under-treatment: fast growth, advanced bone age, virilisation
Surgery and counsellingGenital reconstruction in girls (timing individualised) · genetic counselling (25% recurrence) · prenatal diagnosis

Other adrenal disorders

  • Adrenal insufficiency (Addison): autoimmune, TB, adrenoleukodystrophy, sudden stopping of long-term steroids · fatigue, weight loss, pigmentation (primary), hypotension, hypoglycaemia
  • Cushing syndrome: mostly iatrogenic (steroids for nephrotic, asthma) · obesity with growth failure, moon face, striae, hypertension
