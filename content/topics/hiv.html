Most children with HIV are infected by their mothers (mother-to-child transmission, MTCT) during pregnancy, delivery or breastfeeding. Without intervention about 30–40% of exposed babies become infected; with the mother on ART and infant prophylaxis the risk falls below 2%. Untreated infected infants progress fast: half die before age 2.

Prevention of MTCT (PMTCT)

Mother
  • Test every pregnant woman · lifelong ART for all HIV-positive mothers (TDF + 3TC + DTG)
  • Aim for an undetectable viral load before delivery
  • Breastfeed exclusively for 6 months on ART, continue to 12–24 months; no mixed feeding
Exposed baby
  • Nevirapine syrup daily for 6 weeks (12 weeks + AZT if high risk: mother not on ART or high viral load)
  • Cotrimoxazole prophylaxis from 6 weeks until HIV is excluded after breastfeeding stops
  • Routine EPI vaccines

Diagnosis in children

Under 18 monthsMaternal antibodies cross the placenta → antibody tests can be falsely positive · use HIV DNA PCR (virological test) at birth or 6 weeks, 9 months, and 6 weeks after stopping breastfeeding
18 months and overAntibody tests (two rapid tests, national algorithm)
Presumptive diagnosisAntibody-positive infant < 18 months with severe illness (oral thrush + pneumonia, sepsis, SAM) when PCR is not available: start ART, confirm later

When to suspect HIV

  • Recurrent or severe infections: pneumonia, otitis, diarrhoea
  • Persistent oral thrush after 6 weeks, oesophageal candida
  • Failure to thrive, SAM not responding to treatment
  • Chronic diarrhoea > 14 days
  • Generalised lymphadenopathy, parotid enlargement, hepatosplenomegaly
  • PCP pneumonia in an infant 2–6 months (severe hypoxia, few signs)
  • TB, herpes zoster, recurrent herpes, Kaposi sarcoma
  • Developmental delay, encephalopathy, mother or sibling with HIV

Antiretroviral therapy (ART)

  • Treat all HIV-positive children at once, whatever the CD4 count or stage ("test and treat")
  • First line (WHO 2021+): ABC + 3TC + DTG (dolutegravir, paediatric 10 mg dispersible tablets) · neonates: AZT + 3TC + NVP (or raltegravir)
  • Adolescents ≥ 30 kg: TDF + 3TC + DTG
  • Monitor viral load at 6 and 12 months then yearly (target < 1000, ideally undetectable) · adherence counselling, disclosure

Other care of the HIV child

  • All EPI vaccines (no BCG if symptomatic) · measles also at 6 months
  • Nutrition, vitamin A, growth monitoring, deworming
  • IRIS (immune reconstitution) in the first months of ART: worsening of a hidden infection (TB): continue ART, treat infection
  • Psychosocial support for the family
