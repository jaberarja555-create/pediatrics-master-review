Sickle cell disease (SCD) is an autosomal recessive haemoglobinopathy: valine replaces glutamic acid at position 6 of the β-globin chain, forming HbS. When deoxygenated, HbS polymerises and the red cells sickle, causing haemolysis and blocking small vessels. It is common in western Sudan (Kordofan, Darfur) and among Nuba and Fallata communities.

Genetics and pathophysiology

  • HbSS (sickle cell anaemia) · HbSC, HbSβ-thalassaemia · HbAS (trait): healthy, protected against malaria
  • Two carrier parents: 25% chance of SS each pregnancy
  • Symptoms start after 4–6 months, when HbF falls
  • Triggers of sickling: hypoxia, dehydration, infection, cold, acidosis
  • Functional asplenia by 5 years (splenic infarcts, autosplenectomy) → risk of pneumococcal, Hib, salmonella infection

Diagnosis

  • CBC: Hb 6–9 g/dL, ↑ reticulocytes · film: sickle cells, target cells, Howell–Jolly bodies (asplenia)
  • Sickling test / solubility test (screening: positive in trait and disease)
  • Hb electrophoresis or HPLC: diagnostic (SS: HbS ≈ 90%, HbF, no HbA · AS: HbA > HbS)
  • Newborn screening where available · bilirubin ↑, LDH ↑

Comprehensive long-term care

Infection preventionPenicillin V prophylaxis 62.5 mg 12-hourly (< 1 yr), 125 mg (1–3 yr), 250 mg (> 3 yr) from 2–3 months to at least 5 years · pneumococcal (PCV13 + PPV23 at 2 yr), Hib, meningococcal vaccines · malaria prophylaxis / nets
HydroxyureaRaises HbF: fewer crises, chest syndromes, transfusions · offered to all from 9 months · 15–20 mg/kg/day (up to 35) · monitor CBC (neutropenia)
Folic acid1–5 mg daily (high marrow turnover)
EducationFluids, avoid cold and exertion, recognise fever, pallor, palpate the spleen daily, attend early
ScreeningTranscranial Doppler yearly 2–16 yr (stroke risk) · eyes, kidneys (proteinuria), hearing, growth, school
TransfusionAcute events · chronic for stroke prevention → iron overload (chelation) · bone marrow transplant is curative

Chronic complications

  • Growth delay, delayed puberty
  • Gallstones (pigment), cholecystitis
  • Avascular necrosis of the femoral head
  • Leg ulcers, retinopathy (HbSC)
  • Renal: hyposthenuria (enuresis, dehydration), haematuria, nephropathy
  • Pulmonary hypertension, cardiomegaly
  • Silent infarcts, learning difficulty
  • Psychosocial burden
