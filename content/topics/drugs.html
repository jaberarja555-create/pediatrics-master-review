Children's doses are calculated by weight (mg/kg) or, for some drugs (chemotherapy, steroids), by body surface area (m²), and must never exceed the adult dose. Always write the dose, route, frequency and duration, and check the weight. The doses below are those used in Sudanese hospitals and WHO paediatric guidelines.

Prescribing rules

  • Weigh every child · if no scale: weight ≈ (age + 4) × 2 (1–10 yr)
  • BSA (m²) = √(height cm × weight kg ÷ 3600)
  • Neonates: longer intervals (immature kidneys and liver)
  • Prefer oral syrups/dispersible tablets when the child can swallow · avoid IM in bleeding disorders and polio
  • Avoid in children: aspirin (Reye; except Kawasaki, ARF), tetracyclines < 8 yr, ceftriaxone in jaundiced neonates, codeine
