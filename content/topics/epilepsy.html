Epilepsy is a tendency to recurrent, unprovoked seizures: two unprovoked seizures more than 24 hours apart (or one with a high risk of recurrence). Not every "fit" is epilepsy: febrile seizures, syncope, breath-holding spells, hypoglycaemia and pseudo-seizures must be excluded. The diagnosis is mainly from a witnessed description (a phone video helps).

Classification of seizures (ILAE)

Focal onset
  • Aware or impaired awareness
  • Motor (jerking of one limb) or non-motor (sensory, autonomic, déjà vu)
  • May spread to bilateral tonic–clonic
Generalised onset
  • Tonic–clonic · absence · myoclonic · tonic · atonic (drop attacks)
  • Infantile (epileptic) spasms

Non-epileptic events (differential diagnosis)

  • Breath-holding spells (6 mo–5 yr): cry → holds breath → cyanosis or pallor → limp ± brief jerks · triggered by anger or pain · check for iron deficiency · harmless
  • Reflex anoxic seizures / vasovagal syncope
  • Night terrors, daydreaming (can be interrupted)
  • Benign sleep myoclonus, shuddering, tics
  • Cardiac arrhythmia (long QT: syncope on exercise) · hypoglycaemia · psychogenic

Investigations

  • EEG: supports diagnosis and classifies the syndrome (a normal EEG does not exclude epilepsy) · sleep-deprived EEG
  • MRI brain: focal seizures, abnormal examination, developmental regression, infants
  • Glucose, Ca, Mg, Na at the first seizure · ECG if syncope-like (long QT)
  • Metabolic / genetic tests in infants or with regression

When to start treatment

  • Usually after the second unprovoked seizure (or the first with an abnormal EEG/MRI, or a defined syndrome)
  • Start one drug (monotherapy) at a low dose, increase slowly · second drug only if the first fails at a good dose
  • Stop gradually after 2 seizure-free years (except JME) over 2–3 months

Advice to the family

  • Adherence; do not stop suddenly (status)
  • Safety: supervised swimming and bathing (shower), no heights, cycling helmet
  • Normal school; inform teachers; first aid
  • Rescue medication (buccal midazolam) for seizures > 5 minutes
