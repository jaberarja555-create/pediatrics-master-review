Pneumonia is infection of the lung parenchyma. It is the leading infectious cause of death in children under 5 worldwide and in Sudan. Malnutrition, HIV, measles, indoor smoke and lack of breastfeeding raise the risk. WHO/IMCI teaches health workers to diagnose it by two simple signs: fast breathing and chest indrawing.

Clinical features and investigations

  • Fever, cough, tachypnoea, grunting, flaring, indrawing, cyanosis · infants: poor feeding, apnoea
  • Signs: dull percussion, bronchial breathing, crackles, reduced air entry (often few signs in infants)
  • Lower-lobe pneumonia may present with abdominal pain; upper lobe with neck stiffness
  • SpO₂ (pulse oximetry) in every child with pneumonia
  • CXR in severe or complicated cases: lobar consolidation (pneumococcus), patchy (viral), pneumatoceles (Staph), effusion
  • CBC, CRP, blood culture in severe · Mantoux / GeneXpert, HIV if not responding

Supportive care

  • Oxygen by nasal prongs (1–2 L/min) when SpO₂ < 90% (target ≥ 92–94%) · bubble CPAP in severe distress
  • Fluids: encourage breastfeeding; IV/NG at ⅔–¾ maintenance if unable to drink (ADH ↑) · paracetamol · clear the nose
  • Monitor RR, SpO₂, temperature, feeding every 3–6 h

Monitoring and change of antibiotics

ImprovingFever and RR fall, less indrawing, feeding better within 48 h · switch to oral amoxicillin, total 5 days
Not improving at 48 hChange to ceftriaxone 80 mg/kg IV daily · look for complications (effusion, empyema), Staph (add cloxacillin 50 mg/kg 6-hourly), TB, HIV/PCP, foreign body, heart failure

Complications

  • Parapneumonic effusion and empyema (pus in the pleura): persistent fever, stony dull side, reduced air entry · ultrasound, diagnostic tap (pus, pH < 7.2, glucose low)
  • Empyema: chest drain + IV antibiotics (ceftriaxone + cloxacillin) for 2–4 weeks · fibrinolytics or VATS
  • Lung abscess, pneumatoceles, pneumothorax (Staph)
  • Septicaemia, meningitis, heart failure, hyponatraemia (SIADH)
  • Recurrent pneumonia in the same lobe: foreign body, bronchiectasis · in different lobes: aspiration, immunodeficiency, CF, sickle cell, HIV

Prevention

Exclusive breastfeeding, vaccines (PCV, Hib in Penta, measles, pertussis), good nutrition, zinc, reduce indoor smoke, hand washing, HIV prevention.
