Examine the child opportunistically: observe first while the child sits on the mother's lap, leave unpleasant parts (throat, ears, rectal temperature) to the end, warm your hands and use play. Always plot growth and comment on development.

Minimum systolic BP (1–10 yr) ≈ 70 + (2 × age) mmHg. Temperature: axillary ≥ 37.5 °C = fever. SpO₂ normal ≥ 95%. Use a cuff covering about two-thirds of the upper arm.

Signs of respiratory distress

  • Tachypnoea (count 60 s)
  • Nasal flaring
  • Grunting
  • Head nodding (infants)
  • Recession: subcostal, intercostal, suprasternal (tracheal tug)
  • Use of accessory muscles · inability to feed or talk
  • Cyanosis, agitation or drowsiness (late, hypoxia)

Respiratory system

  • Inspection: RR, recession, chest shape (Harrison's sulcus, pigeon chest, hyperinflation), scars
  • Palpation: trachea, apex, expansion
  • Percussion (dull: consolidation, effusion)
  • Auscultation: air entry, bronchial breathing, crackles, wheeze, stridor

Cardiovascular system

  • Colour, clubbing, sweating, dysmorphism · pulses (radial and femoral together: coarctation), rate, rhythm, volume (bounding: PDA) · BP
  • Precordium:
    • Apex position, heaves, thrills
    • Heart sounds (S2 splitting), murmurs (timing, site, grade, radiation)
    • Liver for heart failure

Abdomen

  • Inspection:
    • Distension, visible peristalsis (pyloric stenosis), veins, hernias, umbilicus
    • Palpation from the right iliac fossa upward for liver and spleen (measure below the costal margin in cm), kidneys (ballotable), masses (Wilms, neuroblastoma)
    • Shifting dullness for ascites
    • Bowel sounds
    • Genitalia, hernial orifices, anus
  • Normal: liver edge up to 2 cm below the costal margin in infants; spleen tip may be felt in infants

Nervous system

  • Level of consciousness (GCS/AVPU), behaviour, speech
  • Fontanelle, OFC
  • Meningeal signs
  • Cranial nerves (eyes: squint, pupils, fundi; face; swallowing)
  • Motor:
    • Posture, bulk, tone, power, reflexes, plantar, clonus
    • Gait, Gowers' sign
    • Coordination
  • Infants: primitive reflexes, head control, ventral suspension
