Visceral leishmaniasis (kala-azar) is a protozoan infection of the reticulo-endothelial system by Leishmania donovani, transmitted by the sandfly (Phlebotomus orientalis). Eastern Sudan (Gedaref, Sennar, Blue Nile, Kassala) is one of the world's main foci; children 5–15 years are most affected. Untreated it is almost always fatal; with treatment over 90% are cured.

Pathogenesis

Clinical features

  • Prolonged fever (> 2 weeks), often twice daily
  • Massive splenomegaly (soft, non-tender), hepatomegaly
  • Wasting despite good appetite · lymphadenopathy (Sudanese form)
  • Pallor (anaemia), bleeding (epistaxis, gums)
  • Darkening of the skin ("kala-azar" = black fever)
  • Secondary infections: pneumonia, TB, diarrhoea (main cause of death)
  • Labs: pancytopenia, ↑ ESR, hypergammaglobulinaemia, low albumin
  • Post-kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis (PKDL): macular/papular rash on the face after treatment (common in Sudan), reservoir of infection

Diagnosis

Clinical caseFever > 2 weeks + splenomegaly (± wasting) from an endemic area
SerologyrK39 rapid test (first line in Sudan) · DAT (direct agglutination test) · stay positive for years: not for relapse
Parasitology (confirmation)Amastigotes (LD bodies) in aspirates: lymph node (Sudan, safe) · bone marrow · splenic aspirate (most sensitive, > 95%; risk of bleeding: platelets > 40 000, PT normal)

Treatment (Sudan national protocol)

IndicationRegimen
Primary VL (first line)SSG + PM for 17 days: sodium stibogluconate 20 mg/kg/day IM/IV + paromomycin 15 mg/kg/day IM
AlternativeSSG alone 30 days (20 mg/kg/day)
Severe, HIV, relapse, < 2 yr, > 45 yr, pregnancyLiposomal amphotericin B (AmBisome) 3–5 mg/kg/day to a total of 30 mg/kg (e.g. days 1–5, 14, 21)
  • SSG side effects: cardiotoxicity (QT prolongation, arrhythmia: ECG), pancreatitis, hepatitis, renal toxicity, pain at site
  • Paromomycin: ototoxicity, nephrotoxicity
  • Supportive: nutrition, iron/folate, transfuse severe anaemia, treat infections, TB and HIV screen
  • Cure: no fever, spleen shrinking, Hb and weight rising · test of cure aspirate in relapse
  • Prevention: insecticide-treated nets, treat PKDL
