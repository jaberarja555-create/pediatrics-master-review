Sources of this bookReferences

Local sources

Pediatric Summaries (Dr. Asma Hadida)Complete summaries of general and systemic pediatrics: the backbone for the order and depth of most topics.
Kibreet Notes in Pediatrics (parts A and B)Short local notes on every system, with the management used in our hospitals.
Aljazeera PediatricsLecture notes from Sudanese teaching hospitals, used for local practice and common presentations.
Neonatology notes (2024)Local neonatology notes: resuscitation, jaundice, sepsis and the care of the newborn.
Pediatrics MCQs (MED 06)Past exam questions, used to choose what to stress; every answer was checked and wrong keys were corrected.
Shendi University cases (Izzat Qaderi, 2022) · Osman cases · Pediatrics cases discussionClinical cases with questions, used for case questions, clues and the "best next step".
Pediatrics OSCE notes · Obaidia OSCE sheetOSCE stations, focused histories and examination routines.

Textbooks

Illustrated Baby Nelson (general and special pediatrics)The reference used to check facts, classifications and doses for every topic.
Case Files: Pediatrics (5th ed)Clinical vignettes and decision points; questions in this book are original, not copied.

Guidelines

World Health Organization (WHO)Pocket Book of Hospital Care for Children, IMCI, ETAT, severe acute malnutrition, child growth standards, malaria, tuberculosis, HIV, pneumonia and anaemia guidelines.
National programmesEPI (immunization schedule) and CMAM (community management of acute malnutrition).
Specialty guidelinesGINA (asthma), NICE (bronchiolitis, fever), AHA (rheumatic fever, endocarditis), ASH (ITP, sickle cell disease), TIF (thalassaemia), World Federation of Hemophilia, ESPGHAN (constipation).

Pictures

Clinical photographs and imaging come from open sources and teaching collections; each one is placed beside its text and checked for accuracy before use. Diagrams and flowcharts were drawn for this book.
